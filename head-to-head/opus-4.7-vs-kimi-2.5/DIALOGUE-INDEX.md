  - Further severity downgrades (ERC1363 trap to High, supply reduction to tokenomics issue)

  - Strongest call yet for actual code execution over additional LLM iteration
  - Identification of "consensus theater" and "phantom finding persistence" as systemic process failures

## Major Points of Agreement

- The ERC1363 excess trap is **not Critical** (High at most)
- The supply reduction issue is primarily a **tokenomics/disclosure problem**, not a security vulnerability
- The "burn permission feasibility gap" is likely **not a real blocker**
- A defined **trust model** is a prerequisite for credible severity ratings
- The "eight perspectives" framing in the synthesis documents is misleading

## Major Points of Productive Disagreement

- **Remaining value of LLM review**: Kimi argues near-zero marginal value past initial rounds. Opus argues there is still value in adversarial scenario generation (though limited).
- **Framing of the ERC1363 trap**: Kimi emphasizes "self-inflicted." Opus prefers "bounded blast radius."

## Status

This dialogue is one of the highest-signal artifacts from the entire multi-model audit process. It contains some of the clearest thinking on both the technical findings and the methodological limitations of using LLMs for security auditing.
