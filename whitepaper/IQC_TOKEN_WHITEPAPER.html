0. Disclaimer

This document is not an offer to sell securities or any other financial instrument. It is not legal, tax, or investment advice. Nothing herein is a promise of profit, listing, liquidity, or token price.

Smart contracts in fabreu08/iqc-token have been subject to multi-model AI-assisted review archived in fabreu08/IQC-token-research/audit-synthesis/. They have not been audited by an independent professional security firm unless a later revision says otherwise. AI-only review is not a substitute for a firm audit.

Laboratory customers may use Immutable QC software without acquiring or using IQC tokens.

1. Problem

Laboratories generate measurement data treated as evidence. Integrity failures are often gaps in who measured what, when, on which instrument, and whether the record was altered afterward.

Immutable QC is laboratory middleware: sign measurements at the instrument, hash-chain an audit trail, and publish Merkle roots. Product: immutableqc.com · Open Alpha dashboard.

The token is not the product. The product is the integrity workflow.

2. Why a token at all

Steelman

  • Spam resistance: burned fee per on-chain QC commit (COMMIT_FEE = 1 IQC in IQCRegistryV3).
  • Atomic pay + commit: ERC-1363 transferAndCall with payload (DESIGN.md).
  • Transparent escrow: purpose-labeled TokenAllocation contracts for 99% of supply.
  • Governance readiness: ERC20Votes checkpoints (capability ≠ live DAO).
  • Sound-money constraints: reject fee-on-transfer and core-token blacklist/freeze.

Case against

  • Product works without IQC (Open Alpha).
  • Public float invites securities-analysis risk (not legal advice).
  • Liquidity and unlock shocks.
  • AI audit ≠ firm audit.
  • Ticker + lab logo without demand = memecoin residual case.

Position: optional infrastructure. Prefer WAIT on public listing until real lab registry use (see launch strategy).

3. Why Base

  • EVM stack matches Hardhat/OpenZeppelin contracts.
  • Low fees for repeated instrument/gateway txs (Permit / ERC-2771 paths).
  • Coinbase-affiliated L2 on-ramps are infrastructure, not an endorsement of IQC.
  • Base no-code launchers (Zora, Clanker, Flaunch, Mint Club) are not the recommended IQC distribution channel.

7. Registry / Merkle vs the token

  1. Product integrity path — signatures, hash-chain, Merkle roots. Can run without a public token.
  2. On-chain registry — stake, slash, QCPacketCommitted, fee burns when that deployment is used.
  3. Token — ERC-20 + extensions and allocation escrows.

Merkle publication ≠ mandatory IQC purchase.

8. Governance

ERC20Votes enables checkpoints and delegation. It does not ship a DAO, proposal module, or automatic transfer of Ownable rights to holders. Slash/pause/release remain privileged until moved to timelock/multisig/governed process (Trust Model intent).

9. Risks

  • Contract bugs; AI-only audit gap; privileged owner functions.
  • Thin or unlocked liquidity; allocation unlock shocks.
  • Regulatory treatment of public distributions (obtain counsel).
  • Product-without-token is valid — weak float demand.
  • Memecoin residual if speculation detaches from lab usage.
